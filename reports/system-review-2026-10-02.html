### Ordered reproducible findings
1. **High—OTP ownership:** verify guardian, change their phone, then request a sensitive document from the new number: previous authorization remains valid. `A/routes/bot.ts:39–44`; `A/routes/guardians.ts:24–30`.
2. **High—false installment payment:** cancel an invoice after activating a plan: reversal credit marks installments paid/completed without payment. Defaulted plans also permit another plan sharing subsequent credits. `A/lib/payments/plans.ts:19,23,46–63`.
3. **High—sponsor settlement inconsistency:** approve sponsorship, then reverse its credit: batch/held/suspense state is not restored because refresh receives the reversal ID, not the allocation ID. Rejected/remainder funds have no further settlement flow. `A/routes/finance.ts:49–54`; `A/lib/payments/settlements.ts:66–83`; `A/routes/collections.ts:68`.
4. **High—preview drift:** preview invoices, change charges/roster, commit: posting recomputes unbound proposals instead of enforcing approved amounts. `A/lib/finance/service.ts:182–209`; `A/routes/finance.ts:57–65`.
5. **High—callback trust/status:** no required high-value/unusual Transaction Status verification; STK becomes “paid” before ledger processing, while UI claims posted. `A/routes/provider-callbacks.ts:34–39,62–82`; `U/components/stk.tsx:16`.
6. **High—delivery reliability:** accepted-but-timed-out SMS is retried without provider idempotency/claiming; duplicates are possible. Automatic WhatsApp→SMS fallback and delivery-status handling are absent. `A/lib/integrations/messages.ts:28–71`.
7. **High—empty teacher roster:** create only a curriculum subject assignment: academic APIs authorize it, but student/stream scoping ignores it. `A/lib/core.ts:23–25`; `U/pages/assessments.tsx:100–101`.
8. **Medium—statement fallback:** same-day date-only range rejects noon transactions. `A/lib/integrations/banks.ts:76–80`; `U/pages/reconciliation.tsx:247`.
9. **Medium—reports/UI:** payment reversal leaves term collections overstated (`A/routes/dashboard.ts:25–26`). Unpaginated 500-payment/1,000-record limits hide history and prevent old unmatched assignments (`A/routes/collections.ts:41`; `A/routes/finance.ts:23`; `U/pages/reconciliation.tsx:61`).
10. **Medium—publication misleading:** published comments/ratings/attendance remain editable/live despite “frozen” confirmation. `A/lib/academics/service.ts:156–214`; `A/lib/academics/documents.ts:48–59`.

## Verdict: FAILURE — bounded acceptance run found an incorrect dashboard billing metric

I completed a bounded browser review against a new isolated synthetic tenant, `DEMO-FOUNDATION-V2` (“Synthetic Foundation Demo School”). It was seeded with synthetic learners/guardians, grades/streams, Term 1 2027 fee structures, and invoices. I signed in through the real UI as school admin, bursar, principal, and teacher; no existing real school or account was modified, and no source files were edited.

### Confirmed defect
- **On `/` (Dashboard), expected:** “Term billed” should reflect the current Term 1 2027 invoices.
- **Actual:** Dashboard displayed **KES 0.00 Term billed**, while Fees at a glance and `/invoices` showed **60 open Term 1 2027 invoices × KES 14,000 = KES 840,000**. The invoice preview for that same term showed 60 already invoiced and zero new invoices. The inconsistency was visible in admin/bursar dashboard views. Invoice records have an issue date before the term dates, so the dashboard may be filtering on issue date rather than the invoice’s selected term; that is a likely cause, not a confirmed implementation detail.

### Verified in this run
- **Role switching:** real UI logout/sign-in worked for the synthetic admin, bursar, principal, and teacher accounts. Bursar saw the manual payment form; the school admin view was read-only for payment recording.
- **Invoice preview/idempotency:** current Term 1 2027 preview returned **0 new / 60 existing / KES 0 new total**; no duplicate invoices were committed.
- **Cash payment and receipt:** synthetic bursar posted a KES 100 cash payment to DEMO-001 (`REVIEW-CASH-001`); the app generated `RCT-2026-000001`, showed “Payment posted,” and downloaded `RCT-2026-000001.pdf`.
- **Ledger and maker/checker:** the bursar submitted a KES 50 waiver request tied to DEMO-001’s invoice; it remained pending. A different synthetic principal approved it, and the UI showed “Approved and posted.” DEMO-001’s resulting balance was **KES 13,850**. A final read-only DB check confirmed both cached balance and ledger net were 13,850.00. The allocation history contains a reversing and replacement allocation, netting correctly; no financial records were deleted.
- **Attendance:** after assigning the synthetic teacher as class teacher for Grade 1 East in the test fixture, the teacher submitted the complete 10-learner register for **2027-01-05**, a date within Term 1 2027. All 10 were present. Revisiting the route/date showed the register as submitted.
- **Report-card comments:** the teacher saved a synthetic class-teacher comment for DEMO-001; the row showed Draft and “Comments saved.” Head-teacher remarks were disabled for Teacher, as expected. The UI states fees never appear on report cards.
- **Reports:** Collections filtered to 2026-10-02 returned the one synthetic cash collection, KES 100.00. Server Excel produced an `.xlsx` download.
- **Mobile:** report cards and the attendance register rendered at 390×844; menu navigation worked. Attendance controls and submit button remained usable without horizontal overflow, though long learner names truncate. A report-card PDF action opened a signed private-object URL in a popup; no download event/content inspection was obtained, so PDF contents are not claimed as verified.
